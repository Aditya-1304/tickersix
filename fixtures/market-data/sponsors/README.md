# Sponsor readiness sponsor fixtures

These files are provider-shaped review fixtures for the optional Pyth Pro,
PreStocks, and Tessera spikes. They are not permanent live evidence, and they
must never be treated as ranked settlement inputs merely because they parse.

The Pyth fixture demonstrates exact target-time binding, fresh-feed policy,
confidence checking, Q9 conversion, and the `solana` payload shape. The
checked-in Devnet evidence deliberately records no verification, transaction,
or cost result. Gate 0B therefore keeps Public Ranked on the Jupiter baseline.

PreStocks and Tessera fixtures preserve representation identity and provider
semantics for metadata and exhibition work. PreStocks entries are SPV-backed
economic exposure, while Tessera T-Tokens are loan participation rights. Both
remain non-comparable and ineligible for rated settlement until a separate
quality and legal-policy decision is recorded.
